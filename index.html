Questions before
boarding

Is a tracker included?

The cover comes with a hidden pocket designed for your own compact Bluetooth tracker. Check the product page for what’s included with your order.

Which size should I choose?

Measure the height of your suitcase without wheels and pick the matching size in our size guide. The stretch fabric gives a little extra room.

Can I use the handle and wheels with the cover on?

Yes. The handle passes through a reinforced opening and the elastic hem leaves the wheels free.

Is it waterproof?

The fabric is water resistant — it repels rain and splashes, but it isn’t designed to be submerged.

How do I clean it?

Take it off, remove the tracker, and follow the care label instructions.
